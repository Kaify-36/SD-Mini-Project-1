Projects

Project 1:Smart Transport Traffic Management

The Smart Transport Traffic Management System is an ESP32-based project designed to improve bus management at stoppages. RFID readers detect when a bus enters and leaves the stoppage, while ESP-NOW sends timing signals to a small ESP32 device on the bus. The system monitors the allowed stopping time and uses LEDs and a buzzer to indicate normal operation or overstaying, helping make public transport management more organized and efficient.

Project 2: Rural District Education & Scholarship Management System

A C++ desktop application built with wxWidgets that tackles two root causes of rural illiteracy in Bangladesh: insufficient schools and poverty-driven dropout. The system lets government officials track school shortages across districts and approve student scholarships, while students register and submit their information through a separate portal — all backed by a nested file-based data architecture.
